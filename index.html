Our Story & Heritage

Beauty Designed Around You

Founded on the principle that self-care is not an indulgence, but an essential foundation for daily confidence and grace.

Crafting Confidence in Bengaluru Since 2014

SLV Wings Unisex Salon began with a singular vision: to dismantle the chaotic, rushed salon environment and replace it with a calm, refined atelier where both men and women receive bespoke attention.

Located on Dodabullapura Main Road in Singanayakanahalli, Yelahanka, our second-floor sanctuary is bathed in warm ambient light and soothing acoustic melodies. Every stylist, colorist, and esthetician on our roster is hand-selected not only for technical mastery, but for emotional intelligence and genuine warmth.

From modern precision fades and European balayage to Vedic scalp oil treatments and traditional South Indian bridal artistry, we celebrate individual identity.
